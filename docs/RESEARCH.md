# Problem research (checked 2026-11-10)

All claims below come from the linked sources. Where sources disagree or I couldn't verify something, it says so.

## Legal context

- **Tennessee ELVIS Act (2024).** Signed 21 March 2024, in force 1 July 2024. It adds "voice" (real or simulated) to Tennessee's protected personal rights. It creates civil liability for knowingly making someone's voice publicly available without authorisation, and for distributing tools whose primary purpose is reproducing a person's voice or likeness. [Covington](https://www.covingtonblogs.com/2024/04/02/tennessee-enacts-legislation-to-protect-musicians-from-ai-generated-voice-impersonations/), [Latham & Watkins (PDF)](https://www.lw.com/admin/upload/SiteAttachments/The-ELVIS-Act-Tennessee-Shakes-Up-Its-Right-of-Publicity-Law-and-Takes-On-Generative-AI.pdf)
- **Federal NO FAKES Act: not law.** It was reintroduced as the NO FAKES Act of 2026 (S.4591 / H.R.8915), adding a DMCA-style counter-notice process. The Senate Judiciary Committee advanced it in June 2026 and it was placed on the Senate calendar on 24 June 2026. The House bill is still in committee. The 119th Congress ends 3 January 2027. [Congress.gov S.4591](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/4591/text), [GovTrack](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/119/s4591), [Manatt](https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/client-alert/congress-reintroduces-the-no-fakes-act-what-s-new-in-the-2026-bill)
- **SAG-AFTRA 2025 Interactive Media (video game) Agreement.** Ratified in July 2025 with 95.04% of the vote. It requires consent and disclosure for digital replicas and a "reasonably specific description" of intended use. Employers must send a usage report within 90 days of release saying which characters used a replica and how pay was calculated. Usage reporting is exactly what Residuals automates. [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/sag-aftra-video-game-deal-details/), [Frankfurt Kurnit](https://technologylaw.fkks.com/post/102mewu/inside-the-new-sag-aftra-interactive-media-agreement-new-standards-for-ai-and-di). These terms come from secondary summaries, not the contract text.
- **EU AI Act, Article 50.** From 2 August 2026, providers of systems that generate synthetic audio must mark it in a machine-readable way and make it detectable, and deployers must disclose deepfakes. Systems already on the market reportedly have until 2 December 2026 (an AI Omnibus change whose final status should be checked). [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026), [artificialintelligenceact.eu](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/)

## A real case

- **Lehrman & Sage v. Lovo (S.D.N.Y. 1:24-cv-03770).** Two voice actors say recordings they sold through Fiverr were used to build clones that Lovo sold as "Kyle Snow" and "Sally Coleman". Lehrman found out by hearing his own voice in a YouTube video and a podcast he never recorded. In July 2025 the court let most of the case proceed. [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/two-voice-actors-sue-ai-company-lovo), [Marketplace](https://www.marketplace.org/episode/2024/10/28/voice-over-artists-allege-an-ai-company-cloned-their-voices-in-lawsuit)

## Existing products, and the gap

- **ElevenLabs Voice Library payouts.** ElevenLabs reports over $22M paid to 10,400+ voice creators. That only covers use inside ElevenLabs. [ElevenLabs blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/22-million-earned-by-voice-creators-on-elevenlabs)
- **YouTube likeness detection.** Launched October 2025 for Partner Program creators and widened in 2026. It is detection and takedown only, on YouTube only, and sources conflict on whether voice detection is live. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/21/youtubes-likeness-detection-technology-has-officially-launched)
- **Detection-only tools.** Pex Voice ID finds voices in music, and Hive offers celebrity voice detection. Neither offers licence terms or payment. [Pex](https://pex.com/?p=358344), [Hive](https://thehive.ai/blog/introducing-celebrity-voice-detection-model). A product called "Royalty Radar" could **not** be found, so it is not cited.

**The gap:** detection exists, and single-platform payouts exist. Nothing combines cross-platform use metering, owner-set licence terms and automatic per-use payment.
